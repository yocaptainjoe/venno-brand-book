01

The feeling.

The love that was lost feels like an error in the universe, with how sure you were of what you had.

The love felt exceptional. You were certain you had found the person you were meant to spend your life with. Even after time apart, those moments still feel alive to you, yet seem like they never happened to the other person. It makes you question yourself and how real everything was.

Central feeling

Give a yearning feeling of familiarity through nostalgic imagery and environments that feel familiar. The music is more upbeat, while the lyrics tell stories across darker and emotional topics. We hope that contrast helps listeners process the good and bad moments in their lives, including emotions they’ve pushed back.

The listener

Someone whose first love lasted much longer than expected. They thought that was their person. But when college and changes in environment came, their other half changed. After years of memories and love, their partner moved on so quickly that it seemed like a way to get back at them.

Then they realized it wasn’t, which made them question: was what I felt a lie? Someone questioning their self-worth. That’s the target.

Album goal

Realize that the love you’re trying to escape is part of memories that will never go away and helped make you who you are.

Rather than being angry, be happy that it happened. Not many people get to experience something as special as what you had with your temporary stranger.
